Hong Kong pathway backlog: IRCC's 2027 plan and what consultants can tell waiting clients

On November 10, 2026, Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) said it aims to process all remaining applications under the Hong Kong permanent residence pathways in 2027, with up to 32,000 admissions. The pathways closed to new applications on August 31, 2026. For RCICs and immigration law practices with Hong Kong clients, the announcement settles the most common question in the inbox and raises a few new ones.

Published November 19, 2026 · 4 min read

What to do this week: split Hong Kong inquiries into three groups (application already filed, missed the cutoff, work permit about to expire), send each group a short reply that quotes IRCC's own wording, and book a consultation where a client needs advice. What IRCC announced on November 10

The IRCC news release ties the decision to the time-limited measure in the 2027–2029 Immigration Levels Plan, which IRCC describes as "proposed in Budget 2026 to support people already in Canada". Minister Lena Metlege Diab presented the details to Ukrainian and Hong Kong community leaders on November 7. The points that matter for client conversations:

  • Hong Kong permanent residence pathways: IRCC aims to process all remaining applications; they "are expected to result in up to 32,000 admissions".
  • Ukraine Family Reunification Pathway (closed to new applications in October 2024): the same approach, with up to 18,800 admissions.
  • No action from applicants: "Applicants do not need to do anything or contact IRCC for their applications to be processed".
  • No reopening: "This measure does not create new programs or re-open closed pathways."
  • Already in Canada: IRCC notes that these applicants "do not represent net new arrivals to Canada".

In the same release, Michael Ma, MP for Markham—Unionville, says that "finalizing all remaining applications by the end of 2027 is an important step." IRCC's own wording is that it aims to process the remaining applications with 2027 admission space.

Where the pathways stand

Canada created the two pathways in 2021 for Hong Kong residents in Canada who had recently studied or worked here. IRCC's closure notice of August 28, 2026 gives the figures as of June 30, 2026:

IRCC keeps processing applications received on or before August 31, 2026. Applicants under either pathway can apply for an open work permit under a public policy that took effect on May 27, 2024 and, according to the notice, stays in effect until May 2029. IRCC's "After you apply" page sets the condition: a work or study permit held within 3 years of IRCC receiving the permanent residence application.

Three groups of Hong Kong inquiries

After an announcement like this, the same short questions arrive by form, email and phone. Each group needs a different first reply and a different next step.

An intake checklist for practices with Hong Kong clients

  1. Ask every Hong Kong inquiry for three dates: when IRCC received the permanent residence application, when the acknowledgement of receipt arrived, and when the current work or study permit expires.
  2. Tag the inquiry with one of the three groups above, so you can count each group weekly and staff consultations to match.
  3. Reply within minutes with a short acknowledgement, a link to IRCC's November 10 release and a booking link. After hours, the acknowledgement says when a consultant will follow up.
  4. Remind filed applicants that IRCC asks them to report a change of address or contact information while the application is in process.
  5. Keep answers on eligibility and options with licensed staff. Automated replies confirm receipt, quote IRCC's published wording and book a time.
  6. Set a follow-up reminder for each client whose permit expires within the next six months, timed well ahead of the expiry date.

Questions clients are asking

Do Hong Kong pathway applicants need to do anything to be processed in 2027?

IRCC's November 10, 2026 release says applicants do not need to do anything or contact IRCC for their applications to be processed using the 2027 admission space. Applicants still have to tell IRCC about a change of address or contact information while the application is in process.

Can new applicants still apply under the Hong Kong pathways?

No. The pathways stopped accepting new applications on August 31, 2026, and IRCC says the 2027 measure does not create new programs or re-open closed pathways. People who missed the cutoff need a consultation on other routes.

How many Hong Kong pathway admissions does IRCC expect?

IRCC expects the remaining applications to result in up to 32,000 admissions, processed with space from the time-limited measure in the 2027–2029 Immigration Levels Plan.
